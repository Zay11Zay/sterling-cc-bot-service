Who owns the morning

Booking concentration at the club's nine most-contested morning tee times — 7:00 to 9:00 AM — across 200 days of tee-sheet history.

Window Jan 29 – Aug 16, 2026 Days sampled 200 Source NetCaddy tee sheet, scraped daily

Reading the charts. Each wedge is a member's share of every reservation made for that tee time over the 200-day window — not a share of everyone who ever played it. On this site a member reserves up to 4 spots at once and often fills the open ones with friends closer to the day; the friends aren't independent bookers, so counting everyone who showed up in a foursome equally would flatten the exact pattern this report exists to find. Instead, each party is credited to whoever is listed first on the reservation — the member the site shows as having made it — and the % of days figure is that member's share of every day the slot was open, as its booker.

A generic "Member" placeholder appearing in the source data (a handful of records with no resolvable name) is excluded throughout.
