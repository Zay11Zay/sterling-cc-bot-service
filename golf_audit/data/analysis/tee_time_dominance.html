Reading the charts. Each wedge is a member or household's share of every reservation made for that tee time over the 200-day window — not a share of everyone who ever played it. On this site a member reserves up to 4 spots at once and often fills the open ones with friends closer to the day; the friends aren't independent bookers, so counting everyone who showed up in a foursome equally would flatten the exact pattern this report exists to find. Instead, each party is credited to whoever is listed first on the reservation — the member the site shows as having made it — and the % of days figure is that booker's share of every day the slot was open.

Household grouping. For the 23 individuals who ranked in the top 3 bookers of any slot, we cross-checked the member directory and merged spouses/children back into a single household figure — otherwise one person reserving under a family member's name on a different day would look like two separate, less-dominant bookers instead of the one household it actually is. See confirmed households below. Everyone outside that top-23 list is still shown as an individual; their households haven't been checked.

A generic "Member" placeholder appearing in the source data (a handful of records with no resolvable name) is excluded throughout.
